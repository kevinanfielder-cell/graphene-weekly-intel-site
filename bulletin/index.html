The week in
graphene.

Weekly evidence points to a dual-track graphene market: early commercial infrastructure is advancing through distribution, production-scale investment and a new facility, while disclosed terms and operating metrics remain limited. Technical activity is concentrated on controlling interfaces, dispersion and hybrid architectures to improve composite, barrier and thermal performance, alongside lower-intensity exploration in energy storage and optoelectronics. Patents reinforce formulation-led differentiation. Overall, translation toward applications appears to depend on scalable processing and validation beyond laboratory performance.
