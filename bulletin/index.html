The week in
graphene.

This week’s evidence suggests graphene activity is increasingly bifurcated between early commercialization and a broad research pipeline. Funding, facility launches and scale-up claims point to momentum in construction, manufacturing, semiconductor integration and diagnostics, although several announcements lack independent validation or deployment detail. Semiconductor and sensor research remains especially active, with advances in photodetection, wafer-scale deposition and quantum control. Across other applications, multifunctional composites show promise, while wastewater treatment research continues to underscore persistent scale-up, durability and standardization constraints.

Carbon-coated ZnO–graphene in carbon-fiber/phenolic composites delivered 168.8 MPa tensile strength, 43 dB X-band EMI shielding and 0.847 W/m·K thermal conductivity. Abrasion testing of graphene–epoxy composites found graphene remained predominantly embedded in epoxy fragments rather than being released as free nanosheets. This provides bounded safety evidence for composite use.
