The week in
graphene.

This week’s evidence points to continued broadening of graphene’s application pipeline, led by thermal management, composites and sensing. Research advances emphasize performance through material architecture, dispersion, doping and process control, while new deposition and metrology work addresses integration and reproducibility. Commercial activity is emerging in cooling, defense supply and production scale-up, but disclosed validation and deployment details remain limited. The near-term pattern is therefore one of technical maturation alongside selective, still early-stage commercialization.
