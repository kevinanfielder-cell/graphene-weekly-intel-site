The week in
graphene.

Commercial activity is concentrating around scale-up, regional access and downstream integration: Skeleton’s Siemens-led financing supports energy-storage expansion, while graphene businesses pursue Middle East partnerships, manufacturing options, acquisition and application-led orders. Evidence for several initiatives remains preliminary, limiting conclusions on volumes or revenue. Research continues to broaden graphene’s addressable roles in protective coatings, conductive and thermal materials, electronics and infrastructure. A recurring technical pattern is narrow formulation and processing windows, with dispersion, loading and interface control determining whether laboratory gains translate into durable performance.
