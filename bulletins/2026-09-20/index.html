Commercial signals remain early, with limited independent performance, capacity and customer evidence.

One-Step Electrochemical Fabrication of 3D Copper–Graphene Structures

A localized one-step electrochemical codeposition route produced three-dimensional copper–graphene structures with quantified gains over pure copper: 77% higher microhardness, 41% higher elastic modulus, and 26.9% lower resistivity. The combination of mechanical and electrical improvements makes this a notable graphene–copper result.
