This week’s evidence points to a split graphene landscape: early commercial activity is emerging in coatings, heated surfaces and sensing platforms, but disclosures on customer adoption, scale and performance remain limited. The strongest technical momentum is in filtration and polymer/composite systems, where high-confidence studies show targeted adsorption, durability and low-loading property improvements while underscoring formulation trade-offs. Semiconductor and quantum work continues to broaden longer-term optionality, whereas production scale-up and corporate-market developments warrant cautious interpretation pending operational evidence.
