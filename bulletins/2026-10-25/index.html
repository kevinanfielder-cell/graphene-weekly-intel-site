graphene.

Commercial activity is broadening across graphene-enabled batteries, thermal management, protective composites, packaging and coatings, with several launches, customer-testing shipments and partnership or capacity signals. However, many reported performance and adoption claims remain supplier-led or lack deployment detail, limiting near-term read-through. Research momentum is concentrated in graphene–metal and polymer composites, scalable synthesis and separation materials, while photonics and quantum advances remain predominantly laboratory-stage. The key pattern is widening application exploration alongside an ongoing need for independent validation and repeatable manufacturing evidence.
