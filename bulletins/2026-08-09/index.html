CURATED WEEKLY BULLETIN · ISSUE 33 · 2026

The week in
graphene.

This week’s evidence points to a dual-track graphene landscape: early commercial activity is concentrating in manufacturable formulations and integrated products—tyre masterbatch, additive-manufacturing filaments, thermal management, construction materials and wafer deposition—while most advances remain research-led. Membranes form the clearest application cluster, spanning PFAS treatment, selective separations and gas processing, with scale-up work emerging but readiness uncertain. Semiconductor and sensor studies continue to emphasize device architecture, stability and low-power operation. Partnerships and regional initiatives suggest commercialization is advancing selectively rather than broadly.
